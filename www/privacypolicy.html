Training OS — privacy

Training OS is a personal training journal. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

Health Connect data

With your permission the app reads sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps and workouts from Health Connect. It uses them only on this phone: to pre-fill the daily readiness check and to attach heart-rate summaries to journal sessions you choose.

The app never writes to Health Connect and never sends health data anywhere. Summaries you attach are stored in the app's local storage and leave the phone only if you export or share a backup yourself.

Removing access

Revoke access at any time in Health Connect → App permissions → Training OS. Uninstalling the app deletes its stored data.
